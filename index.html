About Me

Hi, I'm a Junior Software Tester with experience in manual testing and a deep curiosity in automation!
Currently I'm working as a freelancer in some crowd testing websites where I cover most of manual testing techniques and bug reporting.

I have also some personal projects documented on a portfolio using Playwright and Selenium. It's my introduction into automation testing. That's one of my goals as a QA Tester, becoming a Test Automation Engineer.

I'm a focused, detail-oriented person looking forward to be a part of a development team to grow as a tester while helping a company to deliver a better product.

In my free time I play guitar and synth. I've had a post-rock band, but nowadays I play for myself, like therapy, composing ambient and exploratory music.

ABOUT MY MUSIC EXPERIENCE

I'm a founding member and guitarist of the Portuguese post-rock/metal band JUSEPH since 2010.

Over the years, my band released two studio albums — Novae (2013) and Óreida (2019) — and played numerous live shows across the country, including the legendary music festival, Amplifest on 2015 edition.

JUSEPH is currently on an unannounced hiatus, having played their final show in 2020 during pandemic; it was a livestream act at the now-defunct rock club Barracuda.

Whether this ship will set sail on new sonic journeys remains uncertain, but we never know.

Today I'm challenging myself to play exploratory music, since I'm very addicted to electronic music, effects and syntherizers. I'm trying to find a balance between the two, possibly a mix of both, my grounded area, post-rock, and ambient music, using an alter ego called, Sir Sun.
